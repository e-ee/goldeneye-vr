# Lasersword and Ninja Modes (Quest VR APK Downloadable Mod Pack)

> **Status:** Experimental fork feature on `e-ee/goldeneye-vr` (`feat/lasersword-ninja-modes`). Strictly **Quest VR APK-only** (`quest` flavor / `com.gevr.port`), gated on downloading the companion `lasersword-ninja` pack (`lsninja-1.0.zip` -> `lasersword_ninja.bin`) so vanilla GoldenEye 007 gameplay and non-Quest / flat / desktop builds remain untouched (`VrLaserswordNinjaMode = 0`).

Lasersword Mode and Ninja Mode are mutually exclusive opt-in Quest VR combat modes built around a pure C11 simulation, binary pack loader, and clip audio mixer (`port/include/gevr_lasersword_ninja.h`, `port/src/gevr_lasersword_ninja.c`) wired into GoldenEye's world tick, damage interception, projectile handling, NPC AI hooks, audio mixer, and Fast3D render passes (`src/game/gevr_lasersword_ninja_game.c`).

---

## Quest-Only Downloadable Pack (`lasersword-ninja`)

- **Asset Pack (`GEVRLN1\0`):** Generated by `tools/gevr_lasersword_ninja_pack.py` and hosted in `e-ee/goldeneye-vr-mods` (release `lsninja-1.0`, asset `lsninja-1.0.zip`). Contains 7 copyright-safe baked-shading 3D meshes (`hilt_single`, `hilt_double`, `katana_silver`, `katana_gold`, `shield`, `shuriken`, `wing`) and 12 `22050 Hz` mono 16-bit PCM SFX clips (`hum`, `force_rumble`, `lightning`, `spin`, `ignite`, `slash_lasersword`, `slash_steel`, `deflect`, `clash_lasersword`, `clash_metal`, `force_push`, `shadow_step`).
- **Dual Install Entry Points in Launcher:**
  - **Launcher -> Mods:** Offers both the standalone **Lasersword** pack (`lasersword`) and **LS & Ninja** (`lasersword-ninja`) on the Quest VR APK only.
  - **Launcher -> Rules -> Lasersword** and **Launcher -> Rules -> Ninja:** Include an inline **Get Lasersword & Ninja** / **Update Pack** / **Remove Pack** control directly at the top of each settings page (`gevrDrawInlineLnPackBar`), while toggling the modes stays blocked until the pack is downloaded.
- **Quest VR Gate:** `gevrIsQuestVrApk()` (`__ANDROID__ && !GEVR_FLAT && !GEVR_MOBILE && !GEVR_FRAME`) and `ModManager.isQuestVrApk()` (`"quest".equals(BuildConfig.FLAVOR)`) hide both Quest mod packs, the `Rules > Lasersword` and `Rules > Ninja` sub-tabs, and their settings rows on non-Quest builds. Non-Quest / flat-screen / PC mod hooks are stripped.

## Lasersword Mode (`VrLaserswordNinjaMode = 1`)

- **Blade Styles & Colours:** Choose **Single**, **Double-Bladed**, or **Dual Blades** across 8 blade colours (**Blue**, **Green**, **Red**, **Purple**, **Yellow**, **White**, **Orange**, **Gold**) and select **Right** or **Left** primary hand (`VrLaserswordHand`).
- **Ignition & Blade Trails:** Opening ignition extends the blade smoothly (`GEVR_LN_IGNITE_SEC = 0.42s`). World-space blade motion ribbons (`VrLnBladeTrails`) are pushed once per gameplay tick in `gevrLnGameTick()` and transformed per eye in `gevrLnDrawWorld()` / `gevrLnDrawFirstPerson()`. Dragging an active blade across world walls spawns sparks via `pdphysRaycastAnyRoom`.
- **Tracked 3D VR Melee:** Uses tracked Quest controller poses (`gevrLnGetHandPoseWorld`) and play-space velocity (`gevrLnGetHandVelocityWorld`) to sweep 3D world-space blade segments against NPC cylinders via `gevrLnSegmentHitCylinder`, gated by line of sight (`lnHasLos`) through walls, doors, and glass.
- **Deflection & Blocking (`gevrLnInterceptPlayerDamage`):** `gevrLnBulletTravelToShooter()` inverts bullet travel direction toward the shooter, and `gevrLnCheckVrBladeBlock()` verifies active trigger hold plus blade angle across the incoming line of fire, aiming reflected bolts from the tracked controller pose (`handAimW`). Timed parries within `0.22s` (`VrLnPerfectParry`) boost deflection damage and trigger enemy blade locks.
- **Lasersword Throw:** Triggered by a forward wrist flick on trigger release (`gevrLnVrWristThrow`) or via the weapon wheel `THROW` slot (`gevrLnThrownLaunch`). Rebounds off walls via `gevrLnThrownStep()` and curves back to the player.
- **Force Powers (`VrLaserswordForcePowers` on Opposite Hand):** Wheel slots `FORCE TK`, `LIGHTNING`, `THROW`, and `LASER` (or `GUNS` when `Laser guns` is off). Supports Force Grab/Choke (`GEVR_LN_CHOKE_DIST_CM = 92 cm`), Force Slam, Pull/Push, orbiting dismembered limbs (`GEVR_LN_MAX_LIMBS = 24`), ragdoll throwing, and catching/reflecting rockets and grenades (`VrLaserswordCatchProjectiles`).
- **Laser Guns (`VrLaserswordLasers`):** Enemy gunfire uses GoldenEye's native laser beams and SFX, and starting a mission grants Moonraker Laser (`ITEM_LASER`) alongside your blades.
- **Special NPCs & Ally:**
  - **Rival NPCs (`VrRivalNpc`):** Red-lasersword duelists with Force powers and size options (**Normal**, **Random**, **Giant**, **Tiny**), optional in normal missions (`Rivals in normal`).
  - **Lasersword Ally (`VrAllyLasersword`):** Equips the AI Ally (`#194`) with a lasersword and LOS-checked Force abilities.

## Ninja Mode (`VrLaserswordNinjaMode = 2`)

- **Swords (`VrNinjaSword`):** **Katana**, **Gold Katana**, **Short**, **Gold Short**, **Dual Short**, and **Dual Gold** (`gevrLnWeaponReachCm` scales melee reach; dual styles arm both hands).
- **Ninja Wheel Slots:** `KATANA`, `SHORT`, `DUAL`, `SHIELD`, `BOOMERANG`, and `SHADOW`.
- **Ninja Shield (`VrNinjaShield`):** Off-hand buckler shield (`gevrLnCheckVrBladeBlock`) absorbs or deflects incoming gunfire back at shooters.
- **Bullet Slice (`VrNinjaBulletSlice`):** Fast tracked blade swings slash incoming bullets out of the air with sparks and metal clash SFX.
- **Boomerang Blades (`VrNinjaBoomerang`, `VrNinjaRicochet`):** **Shuriken**, **Wing**, **Gold Shuriken**, and **Gold Wing** fly out to ~`1400 cm`, ricochet off walls (`gevrLnReflectVec`), and return to the thrower.
- **Shadow-Step (`VrNinjaShadowStep`):** Off-hand wrist whip or `SHADOW` wheel slot triggers a timed smoke cloud and speed burst.
- **Evil Ninjas (`VrNinjaNpc`, `Ninjas in normal`):** Katana-and-shuriken adversaries with melee lunges and thrown boomerangs.

## Settings & Ragdoll Integration

- **Non-destructive Ragdolls & Dismemberment:** Enabling **Lasersword mode** or **Ninja mode** activates ragdolls and dismemberment internally in `port/src/gevr_ragdoll.c` without mutating saved `VrRagdolls`, `VrDismemberment`, or `VrBodiesStay` settings.
- **Standalone Mode vs. Full Modes:** This fork stays synced on top of the lean [lasersword-weapon.md](docs/lasersword-weapon.md) branch (`feat/lasersword-weapon`), so the Quest Debug APK lets you test both standalone **Lasersword** (`lasersword`) and full **Lasersword & Ninja** (`lasersword-ninja`).
